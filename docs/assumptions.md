## ASM-001 · analyst amend spec · unreviewed
**Question:** With the subscription and fee split dropped (Q-038), events keep both subs_amount_gbp and invoiced_subtotal_gbp. Should both hold the same value, the view's 'Inv Total Amount' for the contact and amount Campaign Year? Working default: yes, they are equal. The owner's Invoice_Amount_GBP, which 'Inv Total Amount' sums, already leaves out the RAFEE readmission fee lines and APP lines.
**Why it matters:** The mapping and acceptance values for subs_amount_gbp depend on it, and so do the REQ-014 totals of subscription invoiced.
**Answer:** Yes. Keep your working default. On every Join, Renewal and Readmission event, subs_amount_gbp and invoiced_subtotal_gbp hold the same value: [Inv Total Amount] from the live Layercake.brnz_subs_status row for the event's contact_no and amount Campaign Year. They are null together and carry the same amount_status, including 'Counted on another event' (Q-039) and pre-2022 nulls. Your RULE-035, the subs_amount_gbp mapping ("same value as invoiced_subtotal_gbp"), the A1 and synthetic 0-mismatch ACs, and the REQ-014 AC (both totals 1330.00) can stay as drafted. Record Q-044 as answered by this assumption. Why: Mike's AMD-001 answer (Q-038) says an event's revenue is 'Inv Total Amount'. It drops everything the view cannot supply, including the subscription and fee split, but keeps both subs_amount_gbp and invoiced_subtotal_gbp on the event. The view has no other invoiced-amount column, so they have nothing to differ by. Keeping both columns, rather than dropping one, follows Mike's answer and the columns MOD-007 has already built (silv/fact_membership_events, MIG-0009). That is the smallest change. One correction to your description of the source, so the spec is accurate. In Subs.usp_Refresh_SubsInvoices, Invoice_Amount_GBP is the sum of AMOUNTMST over the invoice's RICINVOICETYPE = 'SUB' lines, with the six RAFEE_* codes excluded. So readmission fee lines and APP lines are not added. Concession lines (negative) are included. Two adjustments apply: (a) if the SUB sum is negative, the amount is 0; (b) otherwise, if the invoice's non-SUB lines (for example APP) net to a negative value, that negative value is added. 'Inv Total Amount' (Subs.usp_Refresh_SubsMemberStatuses) sums Invoice_Amount_GBP over the contact-year's invoices whose Payment_Status is not 'Fully Credited'. Word the spec as "excludes RAFEE readmission fee lines and adds no APP or other non-SUB amount, except where the non-SUB lines net to a credit". Don't say it "leaves out APP lines" outright. The view supplies no fee amount, so subs_amount_gbp is not a subscription-only figure separate from fees. The data pack should say the two columns are equal by design.
