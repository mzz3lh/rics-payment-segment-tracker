# Test summary: RICS Payment Segment Tracker

## Test approach

Each module is tested against test conditions (TC-nnn-nnn) designed from the approved specification and design, each tied to an acceptance criterion. The tests are automated tSQLt tests. They run on synthetic data only, in a test database rebuilt clean for each test run, with the external sources replaced by fakes holding the test data. The tSQLt framework and its test schemas exist only in the development and test databases and are never deployed further. Real-data checks are done separately by a person, using the count-only queries.

The MOD-001 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-25. They cover normal cases, rows that must be left out, and duplicates (one invoice number on two accounts; several rows for one contact).

The MOD-002 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-26. They cover first loads, missing and blank values, reruns on unchanged data, changed, removed and reappearing source rows, which transaction rows are in scope, the row-count check, and loads that fail part-way.

The MOD-003 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-26. They check the payment method list as deployed (its eight rows, exact labels and source codes, and no Mixed value); the mapping of known, missing, unknown and differently written codes; and the dimension load's first run, rerun, additions, name changes (including changes only in letter case), a failed run, and a match with the deployed list.

The MOD-004 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-26. They check the payer type list as deployed (its five rows and exact labels); the payer type dimension load's first run, rerun, additions, name changes (including changes only in letter case), a failed run, and a match with the deployed list; and the corporate subscription load's first run, that 'Round' is neither landed nor treated as a change, missing, blank and differently written values, reruns on unchanged data, changed, removed and reappearing rows, how rows are matched, the row-count check, a failed load, and source rows with a missing contact number, scheme number or Campaign Year.

The MOD-005 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-27. They check the line-type mapping load's handling of new, repeated and already listed item codes; missing, blank and differently written codes and invoice types; lines marked as deleted; that hand mappings and other existing rows are never changed, end to end; reruns on unchanged data; the currency list and dimension loads' first run, rerun, additions and new ids, missing, blank and differently written currencies, and invoices marked as deleted; and a failed run of each of the three loads.

The MOD-006 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-27. They check the concession bronze load's first run, missing and untrimmed values, reruns on unchanged data, renamed, removed and reappearing types, the row-count check, and source rows with a missing or repeated concession type id; that the unused dual-membership column is not landed; the concession type list as deployed (the three reserved values and 'No concession'); the list load's additions, renames, new ids, item codes, full-concession and perpetual flags, types marked as deleted, types with no name, reruns and a failed run; the dimension load's first run, rerun, additions, corrections and a failed run; two end-to-end runs through all three loads; and that the reserved values carry the same ids and labels in the payment method, payer type and concession type lists.

The MOD-007 test conditions were approved on 2026-10-27. They check which contact-years get a row, including as-of dates on the first and last day of a Campaign Year and one that no Campaign Year covers; which invoices are eligible (credit notes, fully credited and fee-only invoices) and which is the segment invoice, including equal and missing dates; the payment method from the invoice and from its payment lines; carrying values forward and years before 2022; the payer type with and without a live corporate subscription and a paid position, including double payments and several subscriptions; the concession from the invoice's concession lines, including several lines and perpetual and non-perpetual types; reruns, updates, deletions and a load that fails part-way; and that the two event tables keep their Phase 1 columns and rows, gain exactly the seventeen new columns, and still accept rows written the Phase 1 way.

The MOD-008 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-06. They check each of the four segment anomaly types with cases that must and must not raise it: corporate subscriptions for another year, marked as deleted, or with a status that is not 'Active' or is missing; a Corporate invoice that is not the segment invoice; years before 2022 and years whose values were carried forward; settlement by one method only and a deleted payment line; known, unknown, repeated and several unknown concession codes; full, reduced and missing concessions on a Full Concession position; a year with both a corporate flag and a double payment; and the key and detail of each row. They also check reruns on unchanged data, cases that stop applying, that rows of other anomaly types are left untouched, the load log, and a load that fails part-way.

The MOD-009 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-06. They check, on the deployed table, that silv_payment_events has lost the payment_source column and its default while keeping its other five columns, their types and rules on empty values, and its key unchanged. They also check that the Phase 1 load of the table still adds paid positions and leaves out unpaid and deleted ones, updates and removes positions, changes nothing on a rerun over unchanged data, and undoes everything when a write fails part-way.

The MOD-017 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-09. They check that the member status load lands 'Inv Total Amount' and 'Retired Concession' one-to-one, including a missing amount; that rows landed the Phase 1 way, before the change, keep their existing columns and row count and gain the two new columns, each updated once; that the existing columns still land one-to-one on an empty table; that a change to or from a missing amount, and a change in only one of the new columns, is an update while an unchanged row is left alone; that a rerun on unchanged data changes nothing; that the largest amount the column can hold, the smallest fraction and the longest Retired Concession value land exactly; that a row gone from the source is marked as deleted and a returning row is made live again with its new values; and that a load that fails part-way reports the error, logs 'Failed' and leaves the table unchanged.

The MOD-018 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-09. They check the derived retirement date view: that the earliest Campaign Year with a retired concession and a Renewal Date gives the date, that a retired-concession year with no Renewal Date and a row marked as deleted are passed over, that a later year with an earlier date does not win, that a contact with no retired concession gets no date, with no fallback to the hand-entered date or to 'First Retired Year', and that each contact gets at most one row. They check that the member base takes the derived date in place of apuk_retirementdate while keeping its members, other columns and logging, gives an identical table on a rerun and keeps its error handling. And they run the unchanged Phase 1 loads on synthetic data to check the effect: Practising and Retired on membership events in silver and gold, in the daily counts either side of the date and in the annual rate base; a before-and-after comparison of the events, in which only the status of members whose date changed differs; and that daily history already loaded keeps the old split until a rebuild from the pending date applies the new one.

The MOD-010 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-09. They check that each Join, Renewal and Readmission takes its contact-year's Inv Total Amount from the live member status row and never from the invoice or its lines, whatever the invoice holds: tax, readmission and enrolment fees, a foreign currency, a later credit note, an unmapped item code, concessions, a corporate firm's invoice, or amounts changed after a load. They check which Campaign Year a Join takes its amount from, with and without a paid position and either side of 2022; which single event carries the amount when a contact-year has several, including ties; every amount status, including missing, deleted and empty status rows, Full Concession, Fully Credited, the other invoice positions and years before 2022; and the pairing of events with eligible invoices when the numbers are equal, when events outnumber invoices and when invoices outnumber events, including ties. They also check that Change and Lapse events get no row, that the amounts total the status rows, and reruns, updates in place, deletions, the load log and a load that fails on an amount too large for the table.

The MOD-011 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-10. They check each of the six invoice anomaly types with cases that must and must not raise it: an event with no eligible invoice, including the first year 2022 and a corrected invoice that removes the case; invoices with no event, including two in one year, a Join that takes its amount from a later year, invoices that are not eligible, and 2021 against 2022; Readmissions with and without a readmission fee line, including a fee line marked as deleted, a code that looks like a fee but is not mapped as one, fee lines on another account or on a settling voucher, and a Renewal with no fee line; the edges of the free-readmission window and a Campaign Year with a different start date; the three contacts with two events or two invoices in one year; Campaign Year 2021 and the amount Campaign Year of a Join; and that the event amount plays no part. They also check the load log, reruns on unchanged data, that rows of other anomaly types are left untouched, that cases no longer raised are removed, and a load that fails part-way.

The MOD-012 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-10. They check that every event, of every type, takes its payment method, payer type and concession, and the three carried flags, from the attributes row of its contact and segment Campaign Year, including a Join that takes its first paid year, a Join whose contact has no paid position, years before 2022 and events with no attributes row; that Joins, Renewals and Readmissions take their amounts and amount status from the event amounts table, empty amounts and 0.00 included, while Change and Lapse events get none; that no event in silver or gold is left without a payment method, payer type or concession, and that every value is in its dimension; that the eight unused amount columns are gone from the deployed tables and the nine remaining columns have the designed types; and that gold carries the same values as silver, with the same numbers of events by payment method and by amount status and the same amount totals. They also check that only events whose values differ are updated, that no event is added or removed and no Phase 1 column changed, reruns on unchanged data, the load log, a silver load that fails part-way and a gold load whose silver-to-gold check fails.

The MOD-013 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-11. They check the retirement comparison's four groups and agreement rate on ten paid positions; a derived retirement date on the Campaign Year end and on the day after, with and without a retired concession; several Campaign Years, each compared with its own year end; the rounding of the agreement rate; each of the six retired item codes, and codes that are not retired, including look-alike codes and the reserved values; member status rows that give no derived date; a contact with retired-concession rows in several years; that the output holds only the seven count columns, one row per Campaign Year; that the hand-entered retirement date plays no part; and that running the report changes no data.

The MOD-014 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-11. They check that no employer table, dimension or employer column exists; scheme-link coverage by Campaign Year and payer type, including subscriptions that are not live for the year, scheme rows with no account number, repeated scheme rows and two live subscriptions, an employer on the contact record only where its parent is an account, members with both sources, Unknown as its own row and years with no paid position; agreement between the two sources by Campaign Year, comparing account identifiers rather than names, and its rounding; the top-employers list's choice of firm for Corporate and Individual members, the last full Campaign Year either side of a Campaign Year boundary, the top 25 and a tie at 25th place; that each report returns only its count columns and no contact number; and that the figures the employer data quality note reports agree across the reports.

The MOD-019 test conditions were approved on 2026-11-12. They check that a transaction split into three ledger distributions lands as three live rows with their ledger columns and their own amounts, which add up to the transaction amount while the settled amount stays the transaction's on every row; reruns on unchanged data, including rows with no posting type or ledger account string; a change to one line, one line leaving the source and a deleted line returning; a line with all five ledger columns missing; a mixed set in which the live rows are exactly the in-scope source rows, with no duplicate identity; a ledger account string at its full width of 500 characters; duplicate identities failing the load, including ones that differ only in letter case and ones whose posting type and ledger account string are both missing; a change only in letter case updating a line in place; the first run after the change; and the table's new columns and keys as deployed.
